# TREASURE ISLAND - the STARVED-CLOCK wanderer test (LLM traffic).
#
# The companion to walkthrough-wanderer.txt, and the more important of
# the two. That file is junk the parser mostly ACCEPTS. This one is what
# an LLM front end actually emits when a player types chat, questions
# and flailing natural language at it: mostly things the parser rejects
# outright, interleaved with occasional real commands.
#
# Why it matters here more than anywhere else in the batch: Act I is a
# countdown, and the Z-machine clock advances ONLY on a successful parse
# (CLOCKER runs from MAIN-LOOP under `,P-WON`; a rejected parse skips it,
# and neither M-BEG nor M-END fires either). So this player burns dozens
# of inputs while the story clock barely moves - the countdown starves.
#
# That cuts BOTH ways, and both are tested here:
#   - the player cannot die of a timer they are not advancing, but
#   - the game's escalating hints are also on that clock, so a player
#     who needs help waits three times as long in wall-clock terms to
#     get it. The hints must therefore be worth the wait when they land.
#
# Asserted by verify.mjs: must reach the Squire's Hall, must NOT die,
# and must NOT reach the 350-point ending.
